# 428 ～封鎖された渋谷で～ (PSP)

Chunsoft, 2009. Disc **ULJS-00219**, version 1.01; `EBOOT.BIN` checksum `F4CEFE66`
(`reader.build_id`). Profile: `assets/reader/profiles/ULJS00219.lua`.

## What is read

- **The story's text**, in the pieces the game shows it in (one for each wait for the
  button), with **furigana** and a cell for every character.
- **TIPS** (the notes behind the blue words): title and body, as text of the same kind.
- **Choices**, as a choice: one event with an answer to a line.

A page that is put back as it was (after the game's menu, after a TIP) gets its cells again
and is not reported a second time. What a skip runs through is not read: a piece counts
when it has been up for four frames and its last character has faded in.

## What is not read

- The **guide** (the yellow tutorial box) and the notices of the same kind.
- The bad endings' titles, menus, the time chart, the character selection.
- The drawn dash of an interrupted sentence (the game draws a rule, not characters).

## How the game keeps its text

One routine places every character: `0x0884e674`, with the text surface in `a0` and the
character as Unicode in `a1`. It appends a 60-byte record to the surface's list (pointer at
surface+0x13c, count at +0x140): character, colour and opacity, position in the game's
1280x720 units, and the rectangle on the 480x272 screen. The story's surface keeps its
records for as long as the text is on the page; the count goes back to zero when the page
is cleared. The profile hooks the routine only to learn which surface is the story's (by
the return address: 0x0886c074 a character, 0x0886c3f8 a dot of an ellipsis, 0x0886cd8c a
character of a reading) and reads the list once a frame.

The script's commands are a table of handlers at `0x089c090c`, eight bytes each. 0x1e, 0x1f
and 0x20 end a piece of text (each runs once); 0x53/0x54 begin a choice, 0x5e ends an
answer, 0xbc follows the last. The engine object is at `*0x09ef1e30`.

A reading's characters come right after their base character; readings of characters side
by side are joined into one reading of the word. A line break of the game is kept only
where a sentence ended or the next line is another kind of text (a TIP's title).

## Buttons

Circle advances and confirms, cross goes back, select opens the menu, square selects a TIP
(then circle opens it), triangle the time chart. At the first choice the first answer
cannot be taken yet: that is the game.

## How it was checked

- **Harness** (the real game): Kano's first hour from the first line to its bad ending
  (some 380 pieces, read against screenshots at intervals), the first TIP opened and
  closed, the menu opened and closed over a page, Achi's first choice; cells drawn over
  screenshots for a page with furigana and for the choice.
- **A device** (AYN Thor, 2026-10-06): the first lines, a tap on a word, the cursor.
- **Tests**: none yet.
